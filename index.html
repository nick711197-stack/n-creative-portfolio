FAQs

Quick answers.

Know exactly what you're getting before you book.

Do you film the product for us?

Not in the starter package. Please supply the footage, brand assets and product information you are authorised to use. We edit and assemble the ads from those materials.

Can you run our advertising account?

No. The starter package covers creative editing and export, not campaign management, ad budgets, publishing or performance guarantees.

How long does production take?

We agree a realistic delivery date after reviewing your brief and footage. Complex edits, missing assets or extra revisions may require a different quote.

Are the examples on this page real clients?

No. VIVO, LOOP and MONO are original fictional spec concepts, built to demonstrate creative direction and editing style.

What happens after I send a brief?

The button opens a prepared email in your mail app. You send it to us, then we respond to discuss feasibility, scope and a quote. Nothing is charged just for enquiring.
